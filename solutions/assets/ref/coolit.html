COOLANT DISTRIBUTION UNITS (CDUs)

CHx200

The CHx200 is a rack-based CDU, built for the needs of today’s most demanding HPC and enterprise server requirements. Capable of managing 200 kW of heat load in a remarkably small 4U of space, this system provides cooling for up to 200 servers.

COOLANT DISTRIBUTION UNITS (CDUs)

CHx200

Benefits
  • Manage up to 200 servers per rack
  • Warm water cooling reduces the need for chillers
  • Quick and easy installation and service
  • Install at the bottom or top of server rack
  • Servers remain hot-swappable for service
  • Supports ASHRAE W+ warm water cooling & lower temperature ranges, enabling free cooling & heat reuse.
Features
  • 200 kW cooling capacity
  • Compatible with ASHRAE W17 to W+ warm water cooling
  • N+1 redundant pumps and power supplies
  • 4.3″ LCD screen with touch functionality
  • Intelligent controls with leak detection
  • 4U rack-mount chassis
